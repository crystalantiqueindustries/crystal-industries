Product Handling & Quality Care Warnings

Important Guidelines & Warnings

  • No Chemical & Acid Cleaners: Do not clean antique brass or copper pipes with acidic solutions, rough abrasives, or harsh chemical detergents, as this will damage the protective coating.
  • Moisture & Storage Protection: Store all pipes in a dry, covered indoor environment prior to installation to avoid moisture condensation during storage.
  • Installation Precaution: Use rubber-coated or non-marring tools during cutting and fitting to prevent surface scratches on the lacquered metallic finish.
  • Load Capacities: Ensure curtain rod brackets are installed at standard intervals (every 4 to 6 feet) to prevent pipe bowing under heavy drapery.
