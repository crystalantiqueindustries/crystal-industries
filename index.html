Product Handling & Care Guidelines

Important Guidelines & Care Warnings

  • No Chemical & Acid Cleaners: Do not clean antique brass or copper finishes with acidic liquids or abrasive detergents.
  • Dry Indoor Storage: Store all metallic pipes indoors in a clean, covered space prior to installation.
  • Installation Protection: Use rubberized or soft-grip tools during cutting and fitting to protect lacquer coating.
  • Bracket Spacing: Install support brackets every 4 to 6 feet to carry heavy drapery loads securely.
